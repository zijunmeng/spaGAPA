## 2. 必须主动呈现的弱点（附结构性解释）

### ① 逐点 RMSE 输给平凡基线（B2）

| 对比 | GP | per-gene mean |
|---|---|---|
| MOB st11（本套 P0/P1 数据） | 0.455 | **0.276** |
| 主基准（Fig2） | 0.122 | **0.080** |

**论文句式**（主动呈现）：
> "On entry-wise RMSE, GP imputation does not beat a per-gene mean baseline (0.122 vs 0.080 on the benchmark; 0.455 vs 0.276 on MOB). The gene-level distal-usage index is bimodal, and the mean predicts the dominant mode. spaGAPA's value is not point accuracy but (i) distribution-free calibrated intervals, (ii) recovery of entries that filtering discards (55% at filter-30, at RMSE 0.228 with exact coverage), and (iii) spatial gradient fidelity (0.42 vs 0.00 by construction for constants)."

### ② 逐条目预测力的两种真实形态（B3，比单一数字更诚实）

| 数据形态 | entry-level Pearson | 解释 |
|---|---|---|
| MOB st11 稠密（观察 100%） | **0.737** | 稠密下 GP 有真实预测信号 |
| 视网膜 Stereo bin200 真实稀疏（top-300） | **≈0.04** | 真实稀疏下逐条目信号弱——正是区间最有价值的场景 |

**论文句式**：
> "Entry-level predictive correlation is regime-dependent: 0.74 on the fully-observed MOB matrix, but ≈0.04 on real sparse Stereo-seq bins. Predictive signal degrades with real-world sparsity faster than coverage does — calibrated intervals remain exact precisely where point predictions fail, which is the regime spatial APA data inhabit."

### ③ 区间宽度（诚实呈现）

冻结表 q80 半宽 0.28-0.59（接近 [0,1] 值域一半）→ 区间诚实但宽。
配合 ②：宽区间是弱预测的诚实表达，而非缺陷；分诊（23.3%）把宽区间转化为决策工具。

### ④ 空间分块覆盖违反（P0-3，写入 Limitation）

按层分块 80% 偏 +3.06pp（保守过覆盖）；四象限 +1.54pp；随机 0.03pp。
与 E3 跨组织迁移最大 11.8pp 同向 → 局限条款：
> "Coverage guarantees are marginal and assume exchangeability; under layer-blocked or cross-tissue splits, intervals over-cover by up to 3 pp (80% level), i.e. conservative rather than anti-conservative."

## 3. 改写后的价值主张链（用于 Abstract/Discussion）

```
旧（已废弃）: "spaGAPA accurately imputes sparse spatial APA"
新:           "spaGAPA provides distribution-free uncertainty calibration for
              spatial APA: exact marginal coverage across platforms/species/
              depths/inducing budgets; actionable triage (23.3% RMSE cut at 20%
              discard); recovery of filter-discarded entries (55% of events)
              with guarantees; and honest wide intervals where point prediction
              is structurally weak (r≈0.04 on real sparse data)."
```
